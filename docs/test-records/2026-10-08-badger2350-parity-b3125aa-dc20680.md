## Findings and decisions

1. **Core Badger 2350 parity is physically demonstrated for this recorded scope.**
   Native display, controls, rendering, persistence, battery operation, sleep/wake,
   crash recovery, ROM BOOTSEL and QR behavior have direct owner-reported evidence.
2. **Battery first-frame regression:** `b3125aa` showed an initial `?` battery
   state; `dc20680` (including PR #7) displayed the correct initial state
   on the observed RESET-button retest. Keep the narrower tested-head and
   reset-type qualification; no new firmware change is requested.
3. **Portrait size is not a defect.** 104×176 is the designed 2350 portrait
   size, not an enforced requirement. The owner prefers the original 104×128
   one-bit portrait on the taller display. Valid smaller bitmaps should remain
   supported. No strict-size rejection is authorized.
4. **Battery calibration:** +43–46 mV relative to the DMM meets the reported
   initial ±50 mV check. The LiPo was charging during measurement, so retain
   the existing calibration; battery-only comparison is optional before
   adjusting `battery.cal_permille`.
5. **Gesture sensing is on the backburner.** Do not block the Badger 2350
   milestone or a future product review on the optional APDS-9960 hardware
   test. Existing unmerged APDS-specific development remains separate; do
   not fold that work into parity as a side effect.
6. **Host-specific serial issue:** on the owner's PC, a built-in Qualcomm
   device also claims COM22. A direct USB device path worked. This is a
   Windows port naming conflict, not a badge firmware failure.
